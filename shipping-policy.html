Service Delivery & Shipping

How SHIMORA provides classes and sends joining details after enrolment.

Class delivery

SHIMORA provides public-speaking coaching as a service. Classes are conducted online unless a different arrangement is agreed directly. Dates and times are confirmed according to the learner's selected plan and available batches or instructor schedules.

After payment

SHIMORA will normally contact the learner or parent/guardian within two business days after successful payment using the email address or phone number provided at checkout. We will confirm the selected plan, schedule and joining information. If you have not received details within that period, contact us with the learner's name and payment reference.

Online-class requirements

The learner is responsible for a suitable device, internet connection, audio and video access where required. Any specific platform or joining instructions will be shared when the schedule is confirmed.

No physical shipping

No physical product is included with the class plans currently displayed on this website, so physical shipping, delivery charges and parcel tracking do not apply.

Help

For delivery or scheduling support, email shimora32@gmail.com or call +91 86280 68034. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for missed sessions and cancellations.
